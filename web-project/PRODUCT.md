# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Business owners in Finland and the Nordic region — and globally via referral. They want a professional online presence without dealing with agency layers, vague proposals, or templated results. Often time-pressed, non-technical, and frustrated with cheap freelancers or bloated agencies.

## Product Purpose

Aurealyx is the marketing and credibility surface for Jesse Hirvonen's independent web development studio. The site must establish trust immediately, demonstrate skill through its own design, show real work, and convert qualified visitors into project inquiries. Success is someone reaching out who already trusts the quality before they type a word.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, minimal, Nordic. Confident without being loud. A real person who crafts code with precision, not a faceless production studio. Technically literate, direct in communication, genuinely invested in the client's outcome.

## Anti-references

- Generic Fiverr/Upwork freelancer profiles: cookie-cutter templates, stock photos, zero visual identity, race-to-the-bottom feel
- Minimalism so stripped it has no personality — white space without substance, design that mistakes emptiness for refinement

## Design Principles

1. Practice what you preach — this site IS the portfolio. Every design decision is a proof of craft.
2. Substance over show — what is there should be excellent. No decoration for its own sake.
3. Nordic precision — efficient layouts, nothing wasted, real information over filler.
4. Human at the center — solo studio warmth within technical precision. The founder's name and actual work are the brand.
5. Distinctiveness over safety — this should not look like a template. A visitor should ask how it was made.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. All muted text must pass 4.5:1 contrast against its background. Full reduced-motion support for all animations. Keyboard-navigable navigation including mobile overlay.
